About Us

HERA Initiative is a grassroots CBO committed to advancing Health, Equity, Rights, Advocacy and Climate Justice through community-led health interventions.

We believe that good health is not only about access to healthcare.

It is also about equality, dignity, human rights, a safe environment, and the ability of communities to participate in decisions affecting their wellbeing.

Our work connects these issues around one central goal: Better health and wellbeing for underserved communities.

Our Mission

To improve health & wellbeing

By promoting equitable access to health information, protecting health-related rights, strengthening community participation, and addressing environmental factors affecting health.

Our Vision

Empowered & resilient communities

Where everyone can access quality healthcare, enjoy their human rights, and live with dignity.

Our Core Values

Equity • Dignity • Inclusion • Integrity • Accountability • Compassion • Community Leadership

OUR THEMATIC AREAS

Health is our central identity, powered through four vital pillars and climate resilience.

🩺 HEALTH

HEALTH

Putting community health first. Promoting access to information, prevention, and essential services for adolescents, young mothers, and vulnerable families.

  • SRHR & Health Education: Accurate sexual and reproductive health information.
  • Maternal & Young Mothers: Health education, referrals, and support for pregnant teens.
  • Health Outreaches & Referrals: Bringing preventive services and facility linkages closer.
⚖️ HEALTH EQUITY

HEALTH EQUITY

Healthcare for those left behind. Addressing social, geographical, and economic barriers to healthcare access.

  • Underserved Communities: Reaching rural and hard-to-reach populations.
  • Vulnerable Groups: Prioritizing adolescents, young mothers, and women.
  • Service Access: Linking families to health and social support systems.
🕊️ HEALTH RIGHTS

HEALTH RIGHTS

Health is a right, not a privilege. Promoting dignity, equality, and protection from violence or rights violations.

  • Health & SRHR Rights: Educating on healthcare access and decision-making rights.
  • GBV Prevention: Awareness, protection, and survivor referrals.
  • Safeguarding: Creating safe environments for children and women.
📢 HEALTH ADVOCACY

HEALTH ADVOCACY

Community voices into action. Enabling women and youth to lead health-related decisions and policy conversations.

  • Community Dialogues: Platforms to discuss local health challenges.
  • Youth & Women Advocacy: Active leadership in health priorities.
  • Stakeholder Engagement: Partnering with local health facilities.
🌱 CLIMATE JUSTICE & HEALTH

CLIMATE JUSTICE & HEALTH

A healthy environment is essential. Connecting climate resilience with community health and disease prevention.

  • Climate-Health Education: Raising awareness on climate risks.
  • Environmental Conservation: Cleanups, tree planting, and sanitation.
  • Resilience Advocacy: Protecting health in changing climate conditions.
